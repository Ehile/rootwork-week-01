The program checks five requirements before deciding whether an applicant qualifies for the scholarship.

The applicant must be at least 18 years old.

The applicant must have a score of at least 60.

The applicant must have completed the application.

The applicant must have access to a laptop.

The applicant must have access to the internet.

If an applicant meets all five requirements and scores 85 or higher, the program displays "Priority candidate."

If the applicant meets all five requirements but scores below 85, the program displays "Eligible."

If the applicant fails any of the five normal requirements, the program displays "Not eligible," regardless of how high their score is.
